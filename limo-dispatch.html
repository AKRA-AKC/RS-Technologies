Capabilities

Every Aspect of Dispatch,
Covered Around the Clock

Our limo dispatch team is trained specifically on ground transportation operations — not repurposed from a general call center. Here's what we handle on your behalf.

📲

Inbound Reservation Intake

New booking calls handled with precision — pickup location, drop-off, vehicle preference, passenger count, special requests. Entered directly into your dispatch software.

✈️

Flight Tracking & Updates

We monitor inbound flights and adjust pickup times in real time. Passengers are never left waiting, and chauffeurs are dispatched at the right moment.

📡

Chauffeur Coordination

Real-time communication with your drivers via your preferred method — app, phone, or SMS. Status confirmations, ETA updates, and on-trip support managed seamlessly.

🤝

Affiliate Farm-Out Support

We manage overflow and out-of-market reservations to your affiliate network — sourcing, confirming rates, and tracking until completion. Your standard, their execution.

👤

Client-Facing Service & Changes

Existing booking modifications, cancellations, client status inquiries, and ETA requests — handled professionally under your brand name every single time.

🎉

Event & Group Coordination

Weddings, corporate events, airport shuttles, and multi-vehicle deployments — we coordinate fleet logistics so your team can focus on the ground operation.

🛡️

After-Hours & Weekend Coverage

Ground transportation never sleeps, and neither do we. Full coverage through evenings, overnight, weekends, and holidays — no call rolls to voicemail.

💻

Software Integration

We work inside your existing tools — Limo Anywhere, LimoAnywhere, Ground Alliance, Savoya, or your proprietary system. No migration required.
