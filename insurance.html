What We Handle

Every Inbound Insurance Call,
Handled With Precision

Insurance clients are often calling during stressful moments. Our agents are trained to handle sensitive intake with professionalism, accuracy, and calm — representing your agency the right way on every call.

🚨

First Notice of Loss (FNOL)

Accident and loss intake handled with care — collecting incident details, policy number, contact information, and loss description, then routing to your claims team with a complete, accurate file.

📋

Claims Status Inquiries

Existing claimants calling for updates are handled professionally. We log the inquiry and route or message your claims team — keeping policyholders informed without overloading your staff.

📄

Certificate of Insurance (COI) Requests

COI requests logged with full requester details, certificate holder information, and additional insured requirements — submitted to your team for same-day turnaround. Critical for transportation, contractor, and commercial clients.

📞

New Policy & Coverage Inquiries

Inbound leads asking about coverage options are qualified, their needs documented, and a callback or appointment scheduled with the right agent — so your producers never miss a warm lead.

🔔

Renewal & Billing Inquiries

Policyholder questions about upcoming renewals, premium changes, payment options, and billing disputes logged and routed to the appropriate account manager — keeping retention high and clients informed.

🌙

After-Hours & Overflow Answering

After your office closes, we keep answering. Urgent matters — active accidents, emergency coverage questions — are triaged and routed immediately. Non-urgent calls are logged with notes ready for your team in the morning.

🚗

Transportation & Fleet Insurance Intake

Specialized intake for limo, black car, commercial auto, and fleet operators — driver incident reporting, COI requests for affiliate jobs, and coverage verification handled with the urgency the transportation industry demands.

📅

Agent Appointment Scheduling

Prospects and existing clients booked directly into your agents' calendars — with intake notes attached so every meeting starts with context and no time is wasted on repeat questions.
